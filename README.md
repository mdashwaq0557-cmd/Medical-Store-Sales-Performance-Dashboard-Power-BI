# Medical-Store-Sales-Performance-Dashboard-Power-BI# 💊 Medical Store Sales & Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Power BI Business Intelligence Project

An interactive and professional Power BI dashboard developed to analyze medical store sales performance, revenue, profitability, product performance, store performance, customer behavior, and payment trends.

The project transforms 41K+ transactional records into meaningful business insights using Power Query, DAX, data modeling, and interactive data visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to develop an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that helps stakeholders understand:

- Overall sales performance
- Revenue and profitability
- Product performance
- Category performance
- Store performance
- Geographic performance
- Customer behavior
- Payment preferences
- Prescription requirements
- Business performance trends

---

# 📌 Business Questions

The dashboard answers important business questions such as:

1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. How much profit has the business generated?
3. What is the overall profit margin?
4. Which products generate the highest revenue?
5. Which category is the most profitable?
6. Which state generates the highest revenue?
7. Which store performs best?
8. Which payment method is most preferred?
9. What type of customers generate the most orders?
10. What percentage of transactions require prescriptions?

# 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue Analysis

The dashboard provides:

- Revenue trend analysis
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by product
- Revenue by state
- Revenue by store

### 2. Profitability Analysis

The dashboard analyzes:

- Total profit
- Profit margin
- Profit by category
- Profit by store

### 3. Product Analysis

Users can identify:

- Top revenue-generating products
- Top-selling products
- Product-level performance

### 4. Store Performance

The dashboard compares stores based on:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Sales performance

### 5. Customer Analysis

The report analyzes:

- Customer type
- Order behavior
- Customer-related sales patterns

### 6. Payment Analysis

The dashboard analyzes different payment modes and identifies the most preferred payment method.

### 7. Prescription Analysis

The dashboard provides an analysis of products/transactions based on prescription requirements.

# 💡 Live Business Insights

The dashboard contains four dynamic business insights:

### 🏆 Top Revenue State

Identifies the state generating the highest revenue.

### 💊 Top Revenue Product

Identifies the product generating the highest revenue along with quantity sold.

### 🏪 Top Performing Store

Identifies the store generating the highest revenue and profit.

### 💰 Most Profitable Category

Identifies the category generating the highest profit and displays its profit margin.

These insights automatically change based on the selected filters.
